AI4Aging

AI for Healthy Aging and Aging in Place

An interdisciplinary research initiative bringing together computing, engineering, and health experts to help older adults live well at home.

Introduction

Most older adults want to stay in their own homes and communities for as long as possible. AI4Aging explores how artificial intelligence can make that easier: supporting health, independence, and quality of life in later years, while staying respectful, safe, and simple to use.

We work side by side with older adults, family caregivers, and healthcare partners so that the technology we build fits real lives. Our team combines expertise in computing and engineering with expertise in health and aging, and we focus on three research themes.

Our Research Themes

AI-Enabled Voice Assistants

We study how conversational voice technology can promote healthy aging and aging in place. Speaking is natural for everyone, so voice assistants can help with reminders, information, and staying connected, with no screens or keyboards required.

AI-Enabled Reminiscence Therapy

Sharing memories, photos, and life stories can strengthen memory, identity, and emotional well-being. We use AI to support these reminiscence activities and make them easier for families and care teams to offer.

AI-Enabled AAC for Dementia

Dementia can make it hard to find words and express needs. We design augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) tools that help people living with dementia communicate with the people who care for them.
